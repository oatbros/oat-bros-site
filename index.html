Oat Bros Run Club

Our Auckland and Hamilton stores have a run club at 7am every Saturday morning. We go for a 5km loop at a conversational pace and hang out for coffee and oats afterwards. All welcome, we'd love to see you there. "Any pace it's not a race" :)

Oats aren't meant to be boring. Like most Kiwis, oats have always been a staple in our diet — whether it's a hot bowl on a cosy morning or protein overnight oats after training. We started Oat Bros because we couldn't find a simple, yummy breakfast that was good for us without breaking the bank. All our recipes are made by our amazing team, and we hope you enjoy them as much as we do. Cheers, Cam and George.
